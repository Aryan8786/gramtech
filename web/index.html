        Sell Online

        “Take your rural business to more customers.”

        6 Steps to Start Selling Online

        1
        Create a Product Catalog

        Show your products with clear photos, exact names, weights, and transparent prices.

        📖
        2
        Use Social Media

        Promote your products actively through WhatsApp Status, Facebook groups, and Instagram reels.

        📢
        3
        Accept Digital Payments

        Use safe UPI QR codes and soundboxes to accept upfront advance token payments.

        💳
        4
        Take Good Product Photos

        Use natural daylight, simple neutral backgrounds, and show items from multiple angles.

        📷
        5
        Package Products Properly

        Use sturdy boxes, moisture-proof wrapping, and add a friendly thank-you note inside.

        📦
        6
        Reach More Customers

        Leverage India Post, local courier hubs, and government e-commerce initiatives like ONDC.

        College Project Overview

        Project: CEP Project

        Topic: Technology Support for Rural Startups

        Purpose: “GramTech is a student-developed prototype designed to demonstrate how technology can help rural entrepreneurs learn digital skills, discover support resources and grow their businesses.”

        Key Prototype Features

        • Digital learning modules (Marketing, UPI, Social Media, Safety)
        • Government scheme directory (MUDRA, Startup India, PMEGP)
        • Business calculators (Profit, EMI, Savings)
        • Online selling guidance & sample artisan showcase
        • Rural startup awareness & demonstration stories
